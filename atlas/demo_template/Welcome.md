# Welcome to the Atlas demo

This is a small, practice knowledge graph about **how people learn and remember**. Nothing here was written by hand: ten short articles went into `raw/`, and Atlas wrote everything else.

- **`library/`** has one summary note per article.
- **`concepts/`** has one topic article per subject. When several articles cover the same subject, Atlas merges them into one topic and links back to each source.
- **`index/MOC.md`** is the map of every topic and its sources. Start at [[index/MOC]].

Try the **graph view** (the branching icon in the left sidebar) to see how the topics and sources connect.

## This is a sandbox

Add articles, ask questions, and break things. You can always put the demo back exactly as it shipped with **Reset demo** on the Atlas app's Guide page. Your own knowledge graph lives in a different folder, `universe`, and is never touched by the demo.
